## 2026-08-21
* **Review fixes (PR #4 round, six findings)**: per-finding disposition for the CodeRabbit inline review batch.
  1. *Quantified replacement bounds — fixed.* `replace_count_from_category` (`crates/api/src/yaml_loader.rs`) now parses `Replace up to two/three X`, `Replace 2x/3x X` (incl. `Any model may replace …`) and the bare "any/one" forms into an explicit `ReplaceCount::{One, UpTo{max}, All}`; previously every such category silently fell back to `All`. Application (`models/upgrades.rs`) removes at most `max` carried instances of the target per part (entries are deleted outright when the carried quantity is within the bound). Covered by unit tests for each committed form (`test_replace_count_from_category`, `up_to_bound_caps_removal_at_max`, `up_to_bound_removes_all_when_carried_le_max`). A grep cross-check over all 43 committed catalogs confirmed every quantified category parses to an explicit bound.
  2. *Army catalog weapon rows — fixed.* Hand edits to exactly the three flagged files; every `replace_count`/category retained in them now resolves to an emitted base weapon row (verified by a strict cross-check mirroring loader semantics, after `loads_all_committed_catalogs_without_error`).
